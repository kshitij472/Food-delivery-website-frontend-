Our Story

Founded in 2015, ShivKrupa ChaatHub began as a small street cart with a big dream: to bring authentic Indian street food to everyone. What started with just panipuri has grown into a beloved local establishment serving a variety of delicious snacks and meals.

Every dish is prepared with the freshest ingredients and traditional recipes passed down through generations. Our secret? Love, patience, and the perfect blend of ShivKrupas!
